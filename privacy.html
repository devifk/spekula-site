Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 November 2026

Spekula (the "app") is an iPhone app that shows the weather for a place you choose. This policy explains what the app accesses and what it does not keep. In short: Spekula doesn't ask for an account, doesn't track you, shows no ads and, as the developer, doesn't collect data about you.

Developer

İsmail Fatih Karpınar · Contact: ifk.krpnr@gmail.com

Information we collect

None. As the developer of Spekula we do not collect, store or share any personal data about you. The app has no accounts, sign-in, ads, analytics or tracking. Because weather data comes from Apple, please also read "Apple services" below.

Location

  • Only when you tap "Use my location" and grant permission, the app uses your device's approximate location (city/district level) once. It works only while the app is open and never tracks you in the background.
  • This approximate location is sent to Apple's maps and weather services to find the name of your district and to fetch the weather for that place (see below). As the developer we never see or store it.
  • You can also search for and choose a place manually without granting location access. You can turn the permission off at any time in iPhone Settings.

Apple services

Spekula gets weather data from Apple Weather (WeatherKit), and place search and place-name lookup from Apple Maps. To use these services, the coordinates of the place you choose and (if you search) the text you type are sent to Apple. This is subject to Apple's own privacy policy: Apple Privacy Policy. Data sources: Apple Weather legal attribution.

What is stored on your device

The place you select (name and coordinates) is stored only on your device so it is remembered next time and so the Home Screen widget shows the same place. Deleting the app deletes it.

Widget

The Home Screen widget periodically fetches weather from Apple Weather to stay up to date for your selected place. It collects nothing else.

Children

Spekula is not directed at children and, as the developer, collects no personal data.

Changes

If this policy changes we will publish the new version on this page and update the date above. A significant change (for example a notifications feature) will also be announced in the app.
