Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 November 2026

Spekula (the "app") is an iPhone app that shows the weather for a place you choose. This policy explains what the app accesses and what it does not keep. In short: Spekula doesn't know who you are, doesn't ask for an account, doesn't track you and doesn't collect data about you.

Developer

İsmail Fatih Karpınar · Contact: ifk.krpnr@gmail.com

Information we collect

None. As the developer of Spekula we do not collect, store or share any personal data about you. The app has no accounts, sign-in, ads, analytics or tracking.

Location

  • Only when you tap "Use my location" and grant permission, the app uses your device's approximate location (city/district level) once. It works only while the app is open and never tracks you in the background.
  • Your location coordinates never leave your device. The nearest province/district is worked out on the device. Only the resulting province and district name is sent out (see below).
  • You can also search for and choose a place manually without granting location access. You can turn the permission off at any time in iPhone Settings.

Weather data and the external service

Weather data comes from the Turkish State Meteorological Service (MGM) (servis.mgm.gov.tr). To fetch the weather, the app sends the province/district name you selected to MGM. As with any internet request, MGM's servers can see the IP address the request comes from and standard connection details; these are subject to MGM's own policies and are not shared with us. Spekula is not affiliated with or endorsed by MGM.

What is stored on your device

The province/district you select is stored only on your device so it is remembered next time and so the Home Screen widget shows the same place. Deleting the app deletes it.

Widget

The Home Screen widget periodically fetches weather from MGM to stay up to date for your selected place. It collects nothing else.

Children

Spekula is not directed at children and collects no personal data.

Changes

If this policy changes we will publish the new version on this page and update the date above. A significant change (for example a notifications feature) will also be announced in the app.
